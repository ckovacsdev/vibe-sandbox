We built internal platforms the hard way, three times

Switchyard's founders ran platform teams at a bank, a logistics company and a marketplace. Each time, the platform took years to build and one reorg to abandon. We started Switchyard so the next team doesn't have to start from zero.

What we believe

Platforms are products

The best internal platforms are ones engineers choose to use. We measure ourselves on adoption, not on features shipped.

Your stack, not ours

Switchyard sits on top of the Git, Kubernetes, Terraform and observability tools you already trust. Configuration lives in your repos, so leaving is always an option.

Security is the default path

When the paved road is also the compliant one, nobody has to choose between shipping and following policy.
