Pricing questions

Something else? Ask the sales team.

Who counts as an active developer?

Anyone who creates a service, opens a preview environment, or triggers a deploy through Switchyard during the billing month. People who only browse the catalog, approve changes, or read dashboards are free.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your workspace moves to Starter. Nothing is deleted; services above Starter limits stay visible but read-only until you upgrade.

Do you charge for infrastructure?

No. Environments and pipelines run on your own clusters and cloud accounts, so compute is billed by your cloud provider at your negotiated rates.

Is there a discount for startups or non-profits?

Yes. Companies under 3 years old with less than $5M raised, and registered non-profits, get Team at 50% off for the first year.

Can we leave, and take our data with us?

Yes. Configuration already lives in your repos. Catalog data, audit logs and history export through the API or as a full JSON archive at any time, and we delete your data within 30 days of termination.
