Service catalog

Know what's running and who owns it

Switchyard reads your repos, clusters and cloud accounts and builds a live map of every service, its dependencies, owners, on-call rotation and docs.

  • Ownership pulled from CODEOWNERS and your identity provider
  • Dependency graph from traces and infrastructure state
  • Runbooks, dashboards and alerts linked on every service page
# switchyard.yaml, at the root of each repo
service: checkout-api
owner: team-payments
tier: 1
oncall: pagerduty/payments-primary
depends_on:
  - ledger
  - fraud-score
links:
  runbook: https://wiki.internal/payments/checkout
  dashboard: datadog/checkout-overview

Deploy pipelines

Ship more often, roll back less

Progressive delivery with canaries, automated verification against your metrics, and approvals only where your policy requires them.

  • Canary and blue-green strategies, region by region
  • Automatic rollback on error-rate or latency regressions
  • Change records exported to ServiceNow or Jira for audit
deploy:
  strategy: canary
  steps:
    - weight: 5
      verify: { error_rate: "< 0.5%", p95_ms: "< 300" }
    - weight: 50
    - weight: 100
  regions: [eu-west-1, us-east-1]
  approval:
    required_for: tier-1
    approvers: team-payments-leads
