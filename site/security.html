How data flows

The runner in your cluster does the work. It connects out to the control plane over TLS and never accepts inbound traffic.

Controls

Access

SAML SSO with Okta, Entra ID and Google. SCIM provisioning, role-based access and just-in-time elevation for production actions.

Encryption

TLS 1.3 in transit. AES-256 at rest with per-tenant keys, and customer-managed keys through AWS KMS on Enterprise.

Audit

Every action by people, tokens and automation is logged, retained up to 7 years and streamable to your SIEM.

Software supply chain

Signed releases with SLSA level 3 provenance. SBOMs published for every runner version.

Testing

Annual third-party penetration tests, continuous scanning, and a public bug bounty program.

People

Background checks, security training at hire and yearly, and hardware keys required for all staff access.

Availability

If the control plane is unreachable, running workloads are unaffected and the runner queues events until it reconnects.

99.95% SLA

Monthly uptime commitment on Enterprise, with service credits. 99.99% measured over the last 12 months.

Recovery

Multi-zone deployment, point-in-time backups, recovery point under 5 minutes and recovery time under 1 hour.

Incidents

Customers are notified of security incidents affecting their data within 48 hours. Live status is published at status.switchyard.dev.
